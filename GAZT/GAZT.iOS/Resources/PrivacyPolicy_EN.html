Privacy Policy

INFORMATION THE ZAKAT, TAX AND CUSTOMS AUTHORITY MAY COLLECT FROM YOU

The Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority ("ZATCA") may collect and process the following information about you:

Information you give ZATCA: You may give ZATCA information about you by filling in forms and options on the ZATCA's sites or by corresponding with ZATCA by phone, e-mail or otherwise. This includes information you provide when you register to use the site ("Site"), set up a user profile, and when you send ZATCA an email or report a problem with the Site. The information you give ZATCA may include personal information such as your name, address, e-mail address and phone number, personal description and business information relating to your organization and its employees, as well as, bank information related to Zakat transfer to the relevant Authorities.

Information ZATCA collects about you. With regard to each of your visits to the Site, ZATCA may automatically collect the following information:

Technical information, including the Internet protocol (IP) address used to connect your computer to the Internet, your login information, browser type and version, time zone setting, browser plug-in types and versions, operating system and platform; and

Information about your visit, including the full Uniform Resource Locators (URL) clickstream to, through and from the Site (including date and time); page response times, download errors, length of visits to certain pages, page interaction information (such as scrolling, clicks, and mouse-overs), and methods used to browse away from the page and any phone number used to contact ZATCA.

Information ZATCA receives from other sources. ZATCA may receive information about you if you use any of the other websites ZATCA operates or the other services ZATCA provides. ZATCA is also working closely with third parties (including, for example, other government entities, advisors and consultants, sub-contractors in technical services, analytics providers, search information providers) and may receive information about you from them.
